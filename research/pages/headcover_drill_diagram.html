Where to place the headcover

Your view looking down over the ball at address · right-handed golfer

Your left foot and the target are to the left. Your right foot is to the right. The headcover goes above and to the right of the ball: away from you and away from the target.

Place it correctly

  1. Looking down at address, your target is to your left. Imagine a straight line from the ball to that target.
  2. Move the cover behind the ball, away from the target - to your right in this view. MyGolfSpy's version specifies approximately 6-8 inches in this direction.
  3. Move it just outside that line, away from your body - upward in this view. Do not put it on your feet's side or directly on the target line.
  4. Leave clearance for your club at address and check the arrangement with gentle rehearsals before hitting balls.

Use it as feedback

  • Use a soft headcover or folded towel. Avoid bottles, rigid rods, or raised hard obstacles.
  • Begin with comfortable shorter swings. Avoid the cover, strike the ball, and continue through to a comfortable finish.
  • Do not force a long chest pause or steer awkwardly around the cover. This diagram is a placement guide, not a prescription for body positions.
  • Missing the cover does not prove a neutral or in-to-out impact path. Check club path, contact, ball speed, carry, and direction.

What the research does - and does not - specify

Verified placement: MyGolfSpy says, "Put a headcover six to eight inches behind the ball, just outside your target line." GOLF.com's Nick Clearwater drill says to place an obstacle "just outside the ball."

No invented precision: Neither cited article gives a universal sideways offset or required headcover orientation. MyGolfSpy also does not define whether its 6-8 inches is measured to the cover's center or edge. The dimension guide above illustrates the approximate placement direction; it is not an exact center-to-center or edge-to-edge template. The cover is drawn horizontally for clarity, not as a sourced requirement.

This combines MyGolfSpy's explicit behind-the-ball placement with GOLF.com's outside-obstacle concept. It does not reproduce a source photograph or claim both sources specify the same dimensions.
