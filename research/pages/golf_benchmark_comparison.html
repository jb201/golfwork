Typical: mean of the middle half ranked by carry. Good-distance: mean of the longest quarter ranked by carry, not verified good contact or accuracy. Total uses those same shot groups.

The underlying analysis contains 599 usable shots, with SW/LW excluded and four suspected measurement errors removed. Calculations use unrounded values. Being below a midpoint can still mean you are inside the supplied range.

These handicap ranges came from your pasted table and are not certified population averages. Equipment, speed, shot intent and conditions may differ. Carry is more useful for clearing trouble; total is condition-dependent.
